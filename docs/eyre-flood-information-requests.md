## Letter A – Environment Canterbury

**Subject: LGOIMA / data request – Eyre River flood flows, gaugings, stopbank design standard and flood records**

Tēnā koutou,

I am a member of the public working with the Community Liaison Group for the consented Wrights Road Storage Ponds (Burnt Hill Storage Ltd /
Waimakariri Irrigation Ltd, Dixons Road – Wrights Road, Waimakariri District). I am preparing a screening-level flood
model of the area for the group's discussions. The ponds' design report relies on council flood mapping of a major Eyre
River flood, and the Emergency Action Plan refers to a "1 in 100 AEP" Eyre flood, but I cannot find a flood-frequency
estimate for the Eyre on the plains in any public source. Under the Local Government Official Information and Meetings
Act 1987 I request the following, in electronic form where it exists:

1. **Flood frequency.** Any flood-frequency analysis or design flood estimates ECan holds or uses for the Eyre River
   downstream of Oxford (for example at Depot Gorge Road, Warren Road, Poyntzs Road, Two Chain Road, South Eyre Road or
   the Eyre Diversion): mean annual flood and the 10 %, 2 %, 1 %, 0.5 % and 0.2 % AEP peak flows, with the method,
   record and date of the analysis; and any design flood HYDROGRAPH (shape, duration, volume).
2. **Recorder site 166405 (Eyre River at Trig Road Ford).** The rating curve(s) and their validity range (the highest
   gauging published is 8.4 m³/s); the basis (method, record length) of the "mean annual flood 15 m³/s" and "1 in 10
   year flood 27 m³/s" figures on the site's web page; and the flow record for 2009–2011, which is not in the public
   download (that starts 9 Sep 2015).
3. **High-flow gaugings.** The individual gauging records (date, time, stage, flow, method, comments) for the sites
   "Eyre River at Cross-section IM40" (824), "Eyre River at South Eyre Road" (277), "Eyre River at Eyre Diversion" (822)
   and "Eyre River at Diversion Branch" (820), or at least every gauging above 20 m³/s at any Eyre site.
4. **Flood events.** Peak flow or level estimates, flood extents, photographs, debris-mark surveys and any post-event
   report for the Eyre River in the 29–31 May 2021 flood and in earlier large floods (e.g. July 2017, August 2008,
   1951, 1923, 1905, 1868), including the locations where the river left its channel or where stopbanks were
   overtopped, damaged or at risk – in particular between Wolffs Road and the Eyre Diversion, north bank and south bank.
5. **River scheme.** For the Eyre within the Waimakariri-Eyre-Cust rating district: the defined (design) flow and
   design standard of the Eyre stopbanks and of the Eyre Diversion, stopbank crest-level surveys or long sections,
   the current asset management plan sections covering the Eyre, and any capacity or condition assessment.
6. **Floodplain investigations and models.** Any Eyre River floodplain investigation, hydraulic model report or flood
   map (historic or current, including river cross-section surveys such as the "IM" series), and any modelling of an
   Eyre breakout or stopbank failure. If a model exists, its inflow hydrographs and results as GIS layers.
7. **The ponds.** Any assessment ECan holds (for example in the resource-consent files CRC263186 and related, or the
   building-consent / dam-safety files) of Eyre River flooding at the Wrights Road Storage Ponds site, including the
   "preliminary WDC flood mapping" cited in section 3 of the Damwatch design report and ECan's review of it.

If part of this is held by another agency, please transfer that part. If any item would be large or costly, please
tell me so that I can narrow it – items 1, 2 and 5 matter most. I am happy to receive data under ECan's usual
CC BY licence and to be contacted by phone to clarify.

Ngā mihi,
Thorsten Bux

## Letter B – Waimakariri District Council

**Subject: LGOIMA request – Eyre River flood mapping near Dixons Road / Wrights Road and the May 2021 Eyre evacuation**

Tēnā koutou,

I am a member of the public working with the Community Liaison Group for the consented Wrights Road Storage Ponds (Burnt Hill Storage Ltd /
Waimakariri Irrigation Ltd, corner of Dixons Road and Wrights Road). The ponds' design report (Damwatch Engineering,
Issue 6, 26 October 2021, section 3) states that preliminary Waimakariri District Council flood mapping shows that a
major Eyre River flood north of the ponds "would extend to the North embankment of the ponds". The Council's published
flood-hazard layers (DHI, May 2020) are rain-on-grid results and, as far as I can see, contain no Eyre River breakout
scenario; at the ponds site they show about 0.13–0.15 m of local ponding. Under the Local Government Official
Information and Meetings Act 1987 I request:

1. The "preliminary flood mapping" referred to in the design report: the map(s) or GIS layers, the report or memo that
   describes them, the date, the modelled event (return period, Eyre River flow or rainfall), the modelled depths and
   levels along Dixons Road beside the ponds site, and whether the water shown there is Eyre River overflow or local
   rainfall runoff.
2. Any correspondence, advice or peer review the Council holds on Eyre River or overland flooding at the ponds site,
   including in the building-consent file for the ponds (June 2013) if the Council holds it.
3. Whether the Council or its consultants have modelled an Eyre River flood, breakout or stopbank failure at any
   time; if so the report, the inflow hydrographs used and the results as GIS layers.
4. The basis for the evacuation advice of 30–31 May 2021 for properties "from Wolffs Road" and north of the Eyre to
   North Eyre Road: the map of the area considered at risk, the stopbank locations of concern, any flow or level
   information received from Environment Canterbury, and any post-event review.
5. Records of flooding of Dixons Road, Wrights Road, South Eyre Road or North Eyre Road from the Eyre River or from
   overland flow (service requests, photographs, road-closure records) in May 2021, July 2017 and other large events.
6. Permission to download the 100-, 200- and 500-year depth and level rasters for the area between Oxford and the Eyre
   Diversion for use in a community (non-commercial) screening model, with the datum stated.

If another agency holds part of this, please transfer that part. If any item would be large or costly, please tell me
so that I can narrow it – items 1 and 3 matter most.

Ngā mihi,
Thorsten Bux
[address / phone / email]
