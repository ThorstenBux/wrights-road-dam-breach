## WIL – Emergency Action Plan (pre-construction), Issue 6, 8 Jun 2020 (Damwatch E1154)
https://www.bhsl.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/Waimakariri_Emergency-Action-Plan-Issue-6-2020_06_08.pdf
* Location: **1,534,683 mE, 5,196,928 mN (NZTM)**, ~9 km south of Oxford (s5.1).
* Combined capacity 8.2 million m3; "HIGH PIC"; dam-break inundation maps in Appendix B (maps not
  extractable from the PDF text).
* Monitoring: TDR cable on crest, seismic triggers, automated sirens, level alarms.
* Access via Wrights Road or Dixon Road; roads east of the ponds may be affected by a breach.
* Emergency dewatering (s1.5, App. D.1): primary defence against a developing failure is to lower the pond
  by opening the outflow gates (discharge from the Tub and from Pond 2) into the irrigation network; rate limited by
  outlet-works capacity and by the network's capacity to pass and store water.
* App. F.3: races **MR4** (~6.3 km, ~8 culverts, 4 offtakes) and **R3** (~11.8 km, ~20 culverts, 6 offtakes)
  **both discharge to the Eyre River**; maximum dewatering flows may exceed race capacity (local flooding).
  Culverts are the likely blockage points (F.4–F.5). Dewatering inundation maps promised (F.6) but not published.
* Table F.1 (example): Pond 1 via MR4 at 4–5 m³/s → 7.5–9.3 h per 1 m drawdown; Pond 2 via R2+R3 at
  11–15 m³/s → 12.2–16.6 h per 1 m. Appendices A, B, E, F are placeholders "prior to commissioning".
  Full detail and gaps: [dewatering-pathway-evidence.md](dewatering-pathway-evidence.md).

## Regulatory / guidance
* Building (Dam Safety) Regulations 2022 (SL 2022/133), in force 13 May 2024: classifiable dam
  >= 4 m and >= 20,000 m3 (or >= 1 m and >= 40,000 m3); PIC certified by a Recognised Engineer;
  Dam Safety Assurance Programme for Medium/High PIC. https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2022/0133/latest/whole.html
* NZSOLD Dam Safety Guidelines 2024, Module 2 – Consequence assessment and PIC:
  https://nzsold.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/NZSOLD_DamSafetyGuidelines2024_Module2.pdf
  - three levels of assessment: initial / intermediate / comprehensive (s2.2)
  - both 'sunny day' and 'rainy day' scenarios; rainy day may not be credible for off-river storage (s2.3.3)
  - breach parameters from historical data: Wahl (1998), Froehlich (2016a, 2016b) (s2.3.4)
  - outputs: extent, arrival time, time to peak, depth, velocity, duration at key locations (s2.2.1.3)
  - hazard thresholds H1–H6 after Smith et al. (2014) (Table 2.5, Fig 2.9); PAR = people exposed to > H2 in
    buildings, > H1 in vehicles; no credit for evacuation/warning (s2.4.3)
  - Table 2.6 PIC matrix: PAR bands 0 / 1–10 / 11–100 / 100+ x damage level (minimal/moderate/major/catastrophic)
    x potential loss of life (0 / 1 / 2+). Loss of 1 life => at least Medium; 2+ => High.
