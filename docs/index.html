Wrights Road Storage Ponds – breach flood animations

Screening-level dam-breach model (ANUGA on LINZ 1 m LiDAR). Not a certified assessment. See the repository README and SPEC for method and caveats.

East embankment breachPond 1 → Pond 2 cascade, 7.2 Mm³ released, peak ~2,100 m³/s. 4 h animation. East embankment breach – routed to Diversion RoadSame east cascade breach on the full 31.5 × 16 km domain, 12 h animation: the wave reaches Browns Road at 4.3 h, Two Chain Road at 5.4 h and Diversion Road at 7.5 h, then drains to the Waimakariri River. 69 km² inundated (Damwatch zone 73 km²). North embankment breachPond 1 → Pond 2 cascade through the Dixon Road embankment, 4.7 Mm³ released, peak ~820 m³/s. The flow turns east north of the east-breach corridor. 4 h animation. North embankment breach – routed to Diversion RoadSame north breach on the full 31.5 × 16 km domain, 12 h animation: Downs Road at 4.5 h, Browns Road at 5.9 h, Two Chain Road at 7.4 h and Diversion Road at 9.6 h. 49 km² inundated (Damwatch zone 66 km²). West embankment breachPond 1 piping failure only, 1.2 Mm³ released, peak ~235 m³/s. 4 h animation. Earthquake – all embankments fail at oncePostulated seismic failure at full supply (Alpine Fault Mw 8 or Darfield-type Mw 7 shaking): east, south, north, west and dividing embankments breach simultaneously with no warning. 7.4 Mm³ released, combined peak ~3,000 m³/s within 35 min. 4 h animation. Storm – breach during heavy rain, Eyre River in floodRainy-day combination: the east cascade breach opens after 2 h of 10 mm/h rain on saturated ground with the Eyre River already carrying 150 m³/s (assumed) where it crosses the flood corridor. Same breach as East, wetter plain, no river capacity to spare. 4 h animation after the breach.
